1. Our independent business role

TIMEDEFINITION LIMITED operates online lead-generation, customer-acquisition and digital marketing activity. Our role may include creating informational or promotional content, identifying prospective customer interest and referring users to independent third-party service providers.

TIMEDEFINITION LIMITED is responsible for the lead generation and digital marketing services it provides. Independent third-party providers remain responsible for any separate products or services supplied directly by them.

Unless an applicable commercial relationship is expressly stated, TIMEDEFINITION LIMITED does not claim to own, operate, act as authorised agent for, or officially represent third-party businesses referenced through our marketing, and does not supply their products or services.

2. Compensation and commercial relationships

TIMEDEFINITION LIMITED may receive fees, commissions or other compensation for qualifying enquiries, leads, referrals, customer-acquisition services, registrations, purchases or other agreed marketing outcomes. The method and amount of compensation may vary by campaign or provider.

Commercial compensation may influence which providers, services or offers are featured. It does not transfer responsibility for the underlying third-party product or service to TIMEDEFINITION LIMITED.

3. Responsibility for services

TIMEDEFINITION LIMITED is responsible for the lead generation and digital marketing services it provides. Independent third-party providers remain responsible for any separate products or services supplied directly by them, including their own:

  • products and services;
  • advertised features and eligibility criteria;
  • prices, fees and payment processing;
  • account registration and contractual terms;
  • delivery or fulfilment; and
  • customer service, refunds and dispute handling.

Where you follow a link to another website or begin a relationship with a third-party provider, you should review that provider's current terms, privacy information and other disclosures.

4. Brand names, logos and trademarks

Third-party company names, product names, logos and trademarks are the property of their respective owners. Reference to a brand or service does not by itself indicate sponsorship, endorsement, ownership, agency or an official partnership with TIMEDEFINITION LIMITED.

5. User decisions

Users remain responsible for deciding whether a third-party product or service is suitable for their needs. Material commercial information should be checked directly with the relevant provider before registering, purchasing or entering into an agreement.
